Five ways to be understood

One app for every moment.

Whether it's an auto ride, a pharmacy counter, a railway announcement or a restaurant menu, Tarang has a mode for it.

Conversation

A split screen with one half each. Speak and Tarang says the translation out loud. Face-to-face mode flips the other half so they can read it from across the table, with prompts in their own script.

Live captions

Rolling subtitles for announcements, lectures and guides. They update while people are still talking.

Type

Type, paste or dictate, and the translation appears as you go. Show it full screen to anyone.

Scan

Point at a sign, menu or notice. The text is read on your phone, then translated.

Phrasebook

48 travel essentials for greetings, food, shopping, health and emergencies. Tap a phrase to speak it.

How to install

Download the APK

Tap Download APK. Most phones need the arm64 build. If you're not sure, get the universal APK.

Allow the install

Open the file. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser.

Pick your languages

Choose the language you speak and the language of the person you're talking with, then grab the offline packs.

Questions

Is Tarang really free?

Yes. It runs on free models from Google ML Kit and Android's built-in speech services. There are no ads, accounts or in-app purchases, and the source code is on GitHub.

Does it work without internet?

Translation, text-to-speech and photo scanning work fully offline once you download the language pack (~30 MB each). For offline voice input, add your languages under Android's voice typing settings. Tarang links you there.

Which languages are supported?

Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu and Kannada are the eight most spoken languages in India. English is included too. Any of them can be translated into any other.

Is my voice or text sent anywhere?

On-device translation and scanning never leave your phone. Only if you use the optional online engine is the text being translated sent to MyMemory. Choose Offline mode to prevent that. See the privacy notes.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Not yet! For now, install the APK directly from GitHub Releases. Each release is built automatically from the public source code.
