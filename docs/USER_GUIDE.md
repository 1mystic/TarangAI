# Tarang user guide

## First launch
1. Tap **Get started**.
2. Choose **the language you speak**, then **the language of the person you want to talk with**.
3. Tap **Download & start** to get the offline packs (about 30 MB each, Wi-Fi only by default), or **Later** to use
   the free online engine for now.
4. Allow the microphone when asked.

## Conversation
* The **bottom half is yours** and the **top half is theirs**. Each half shows the whole conversation in that
  person's language.
* Tap the mic, speak, and tap again to stop (or just pause). Tarang translates and **speaks the result aloud**.
* The prompt under each mic is written in that person's language, so they know what to do.
* Tap **⟲ (face-to-face)** to flip their half upside-down when you're sitting across from each other.
* Tap any translated bubble to hear it again.

## Live captions
* Set **Listen to** (their language) and **Show in** (yours), then tap the mic.
* Captions roll continuously. The faint line at the bottom is what's being said right now.
* **A− / A+** change the text size. **Read aloud** speaks each caption and pauses listening while it does.

## Type
* Type, paste or tap the mic to dictate. The translation appears as you type.
* Tap **🔊** to listen, **⧉** to copy, **share**, **☆** to keep it in Favourites, or **⛶** to show it full screen to
  someone.
* **Swap** moves the translation into the input box, which is handy for replies.
* In any app, select text and choose **Translate with Tarang** from the menu.

## Scan
* Tap **Camera** or **Gallery**. Tarang reads the text on your phone, and you can correct it before translating.
* Scan works with English, Hindi and Marathi text (Latin and Devanagari scripts).

## Phrasebook
* Pick a category. Each phrase shows in their language (large) and yours (small).
* **Tap** a phrase to speak it, or **long-press** to show it full screen.

## Make everything work offline
1. **Translation:** Settings → *Offline language packs* → download your languages.
2. **Speech recognition:** Settings → *Voice typing languages* → *Offline speech recognition* → add your languages.
   Then turn on *Prefer offline speech recognition* in Tarang.
3. **Voices:** Settings → *Voices & offline speech* → install voice data for your languages.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Speech needs internet for …" | Install that language's offline speech pack (step 2 above) or connect to the internet. |
| "No … voice installed" | Install the voice in your text-to-speech settings (step 3 above). Speech Services by Google includes all 9 languages. |
| "Download the … pack to translate offline" | You're in *Offline* engine mode. Download the pack or switch the engine to *Smart*. |
| Online translations stop | The free online engine allows about 5,000 characters a day. Download packs for unlimited offline use. |
| Mic doesn't start | Make sure the **Google** app is installed and enabled, and that the microphone permission is granted. |
